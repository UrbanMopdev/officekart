Photo-scanned skies, ground textures and models in this folder come from
Poly Haven (https://polyhaven.com), which publishes all of its assets under
CC0 1.0 (public domain): https://polyhaven.com/license

They have been resized, re-encoded and in some cases colour-graded or
simplified for the game. Poly Haven asset names:

  sky/day.*, sky/ember.*      kloppenheim_06_puresky (ember is a warm grade of it)
  sky/night.*                 kloppenheim_02_puresky
  tex/asphalt_*               asphalt_track
  tex/snow_*                  snow_02
  tex/cliff_*                 cliff_side
  tex/basalt_*                rock_wall_02
  tex/ash_*                   rocky_terrain_02
  tex/bark_*                  pine_bark
  tex/grass_*                 leafy_grass
  models/barrier*             concrete_road_barrier
  models/namaqualand_boulder_02*   namaqualand_boulder_02
  models/rock_09*             rock_09
